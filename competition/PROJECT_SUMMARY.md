# Project summary - AURORA-SR

AURORA-SR is an Earth-observation research project that develops and evaluates models for spectral reconstruction and drought analysis. Its main research combines climate history with land-surface observations to explore environmental change. NASA POWER is the primary climate source: daily corrected precipitation and mean, maximum, and minimum air temperature are processed into rolling climate features, then aligned with Sentinel-2 scenes. The following 30 days of rainfall define an exploratory rainfall-deficit drought target. Sentinel-2 contributes vegetation and moisture signals, while SpectralNetV10 reconstructs six Sentinel-2-style bands from RGB imagery. The website makes selected research outputs accessible and adds a bounded NASA POWER Trend Lab, where visitors compare annual climate trends across four Indian grid points. The site uses a precomputed record and does not run the full research or training pipeline live. AURORA-SR supports transparent, exploratory analysis while stating the limits of model outputs and proxy labels.
