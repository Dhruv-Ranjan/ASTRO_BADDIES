Interpretation

The drought index is a composite ranking signal combining a classifier output and physics-derived spectral stress. The confidence head is explicitly uncalibrated and must not be read as a probability of correctness.

The 30-day target spectrum is a linear extrapolation and the training labels are predominantly proxy-derived.

AURORA mission control

auditable browser replay

Adjust resources and run the same candidate-ranking logic as `src/integrated_mission.py`: information gain is weighted by drought risk and uncertainty, cloud risk reduces value, and utility is information gain per energy.

The public interactive bundle does not include the full baseline-policy result tables from the research simulator. It will not invent a comparison. The reproducible offline AURORA-SR experiment and its figures remain in the release research package.

Scientific limitations

  • Validation is not geographically disjoint.
  • Drought labels are predominantly proxy-derived where independent TerraClimate labels are unavailable.
  • Some datasets and checkpoints are excluded from GitHub because of size.
  • Mission resources, access windows, cloud probability, and belief updates are simulation constructs.
  • This is not an operational satellite system, flight software, NASA endorsement, or a substitute for field measurements.
